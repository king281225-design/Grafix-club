      Good to know

      Quick answers.

      How long does a custom neon sign take?

      Most neon signs are ready within a few working days after design approval. Large or complex designs can take longer. We confirm the date when we quote.

      Can I get a free design before I pay?

      Yes. Send your text, logo or idea on WhatsApp and we will share a mock-up and price before you commit.

      Do you make plates for bikes and cars?

      Yes. We make 3D acrylic plates in several finishes. Decorative plates are meant for display and personal use. Please make sure what you fit on a road vehicle follows local HSRP and transport rules.

      Which payment methods do you accept?

      UPI and cash.
